ROLLING AVERAGES & SENSOR TRENDS

1. What is the purpose of a rolling average?

A rolling average smooths short-term fluctuations and noise in sensor
readings, making the overall trend easier to observe.

2. What did the 5-reading rolling average show?

The rolling average produced a smoother representation of temperature
and RPM compared with the original sensor readings. This makes broader
changes in sensor behavior easier to observe.

3. If temperature slowly increases for 30 minutes, is that more useful
than one single high temperature reading?

Yes. A sustained increase may indicate a developing trend in motor
behavior, while a single high reading could result from temporary noise,
a short-term load change or measurement error. A continuous trend can
therefore provide more useful information for predictive maintenance.

4. Why are rolling averages useful for motor monitoring?

They reduce the influence of small fluctuations and help reveal
underlying trends. This can support anomaly detection and feature
engineering before Machine Learning is applied.
