WEEK 5 – DAY 4
ANOMALY DETECTION

1. What makes a reading an anomaly?

A reading can be considered an anomaly when it significantly differs
from expected or defined normal behavior. In this exercise, a reading
was flagged when Temperature exceeded 75, RPM was below 1420, or Current
exceeded 9.5.

2. Can every anomaly mean that the motor is failing?

No. An anomaly only indicates unusual behavior. It may be caused by
temporary operating conditions, sensor noise, measurement errors,
environmental changes, or an actual developing fault.

3. What other reasons could cause an unusual reading?

Unusual readings may result from sensor malfunction, electrical
interference, sudden load changes, motor startup or shutdown, environmental
conditions, communication errors, or data-processing errors.

4. Why could anomaly detection be useful in predictive maintenance?

Anomaly detection can identify unusual motor behavior early and alert
operators to investigate the condition before it develops into a more
serious fault or unexpected failure.
