Observations from Motor Health Analysis
The motor sensor data showed fluctuations in temperature, current, and RPM throughout the recorded period.
Several readings exceeded the selected temperature and current thresholds, while some RPM readings fell below the selected minimum.
The health classification demonstrated how multiple sensor abnormalities can be combined to determine an overall motor condition.
A single abnormal sensor reading was classified as WARNING, while two or more abnormal readings were classified as CRITICAL.
The analysis demonstrates the basic principle of condition monitoring, where sensor data is continuously evaluated to identify possible abnormal operating conditions.


Why these thresholds were chosen?

=> Important: These thresholds were selected for programming and learning purposes only. They are not scientifically validated motor safety limits.

Parameter	Exercise Threshold	Reason
Temperature	> 70°C	Chosen to demonstrate detection of elevated motor temperature.
Current	> 9.5 A	Chosen to demonstrate detection of unusually high current consumption.
RPM	< 1450 RPM	Chosen to demonstrate detection of reduced motor speed.
